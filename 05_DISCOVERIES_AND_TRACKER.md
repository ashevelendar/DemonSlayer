## KNOWN DISCOVERIES

- Ashe has been transformed into a demon.
- Muzan Kibutsuji is the demon responsible for Ashe's transformation.
- Demons possess supernatural abilities and severe weaknesses, including vulnerability to sunlight.
- Demons are hunted by Demon Slayers.
- A hierarchy exists among demons, including Upper Ranks.
- Ashe currently has no established Blood Demon Art.
- Feeding on humans satisfies Ashe's hunger and has produced a noticeable, if still subtle, increase in physical capability.
- Ashe's singing can produce an unusual emotional influence over frightened listeners.
- The emerging effect has been observed to draw a listener toward willing surrender.

## ACTIVE INVESTIGATIONS

### Ashe's New Body
**Subject:** Discovering the practical limits of Ashe's demon physiology.  
**Known Evidence:** Ashe has confirmed heightened senses, increased speed and agility, increased strength, rapid healing from cuts, severe hunger, and feeding-related physical improvement.  
**Unknowns:** Durability, regeneration limits, feeding growth curve, and other capabilities.  
**Leads:** Direct experimentation.  
**People Involved:** Ashe.  
**Locations Involved:** Mountain Village, Kii.  
**Current Status:** Active.

### Resonance
**Subject:** Understanding the supernatural effect emerging through Ashe's singing.  
**Known Evidence:** Singing has calmed frightened survivors and made surrender feel emotionally acceptable; one young survivor voluntarily approached Ashe after the song.  
**Unknowns:** Mechanism, range, duration, resistance, whether it can affect multiple targets, and whether it can develop into a Blood Demon Art.  
**Leads:** Controlled experimentation after securing daytime shelter.  
**People Involved:** Ashe.  
**Locations Involved:** Mountain Village, Kii.  
**Current Status:** Active.
