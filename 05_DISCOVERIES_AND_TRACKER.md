The village became one continuous Resonant structure. Every house has a blood-soaked stone near its foundation, with additional vessels forming the connections and emotional atmosphere.

A large boulder was immersed in Ashe's blood for a month, with more blood added every day. The villagers then moved it into the middle of Mizunashi. Ashe designated the Heart as her public "Inn" and sole public singing place.
