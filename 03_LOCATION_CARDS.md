# LOCATION CARDS

## MOUNTAIN VILLAGE, KII

Ashe's birthplace. A remote mountain settlement in Kii Province where the original massacre occurred. Ashe no longer lives here.

## MIZUNASHI VILLAGE

### Overview
A separate rural settlement in Kii Province. Ashe established herself here after leaving her birthplace.

### Environment
Remote wooded village with an inn, surrounding forest, mountain paths, and occasional traveller traffic.

### Population
Roughly 90.

### Authority
Village elders and household heads.

### Economy
Farming, hunting, small trade, and traveller traffic.

### Current Situation
Ashe is currently based here under her public identity as a singer. Her weekly performances became a major village tradition. The Songbound faction has 29 permanent members. Hana, Mika, and Daichi form the primary Anchor tier, with the remaining Songbound acting as secondary Anchors.

Repeated exposure through common ale, baked goods, well water, and performances has established permanent Resonance connections throughout the entire village. The network varies in strength and Ashe cannot consciously process every individual at once, but the hierarchy allows the network to remain stable without continuous singing.

### Dangers
Sunlight, wild animals, possible Demon Slayer attention, outside travellers, and the danger of Ashe's feeding activities being discovered.

### Player Relationship
Current settlement and centre of Ashe's social influence and developing Blood Demon Art.

## ASHE'S FAMILY HOUSE

### Overview
Ashe's former family home in her birthplace.

### Important Feature
Subterranean basement suitable as daytime shelter.

### Current Situation
No longer Ashe's current residence. It remains a potential hidden shelter and is separate from Mizunashi Village.

### Player Relationship
Former home and fallback shelter.
